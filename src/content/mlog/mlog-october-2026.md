## Music

### Prince

Listening in and around his discography, particularly his 90s stuff after he changed his name to the Love Symbol. Come and Jam of the The Year have been on repeat in my head.

### Black Milk

I went to see him on 16th and managed to chat to him briefly after the gig (he remembered me from when I interviewed him in 2014 so that was really cool). The gig was awesome; a mix of raps and beats and everything was on point. Great atmosphere. I got a copy of his new album and he signed it and took a photo with me to go with the one from 2014. I think I look better now than I did then.
