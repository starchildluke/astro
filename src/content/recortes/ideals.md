i was watching a time lapse of someone making a 3d model of a super nintendo controller and the level of detail and time he put into it was incredible. but it got me thinking about that kind of work in an ai-driven age where too many people would suggest to just use claude/chatgpt/whatever instead.

and it made me realise that what we need beyond the usual "destroy all data centres" rhetoric is:

- a universal basic income—and a high one at that
- better salaries for all
- a rehaul of the time culture we live in (ie. understanding that things take time and we have the time to wait)
- reworking our language around cost—instead of something being expensive, realising that maybe the price is okay but we just can't afford it right now (caveats may apply!)
- treat service workers with higher levels of respect or dignity than we currently do
- support people's creativity by offering words of encouragement and validation and sharing their work

idealistic? you betcha! but where would we be without ideals? if we had things like the above, we'd be able to know that someone took hours to make a thing, maybe sell it for a price, and we could either buy it knowing that we got something amazing and helped someone live and enjoy their life, or wait until we could afford it and/or passing it on to someone who could.
