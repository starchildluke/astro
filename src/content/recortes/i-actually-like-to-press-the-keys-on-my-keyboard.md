aside from the attention seeking nature of vibe coded projects proliferating the scene right now, projects have always been about prioritisation and discernment.

what we have now are half-baked apps riddled with unknown bugs because vibe coding requires you not to check for anything. the original concept wasn't meant for production ready apps but people are treating them as such and then asking for human contributions to fix it. i recently saw someone proudly brandishing their recent decomp project for one of my favourite video games and people were defending him because apparently he'd been feeling burn out and this helped him out of it. well shit, congrats but look at all the unemployed tech workers out there not cutting corners to get paid ANYTHING for a career they've spent years building. burnout sucks but it's not a reason or an excuse. there is no defence here.

i'm sure someone has asked why you'd bother sculpting now if you can prompt an LLM to give you a 3D model to 3D print. why do maths if LLMs can solve the problems? why drive? why make music? why hire a lawyer? why take photos of your house to rent/sell it? a bunch of non-existent problems with egocentric solutions that turn into everyone else's problem in the end. GitHub is a graveyard of undercooked what if manifestations. frankensteinian code that no well-meaning scientist would ever touch in good faith. and this is the bright future ahead of us? Actually, no. as they love to tell us, it's already here and we just have to embrace it or turn into a pile of dust.

no thanks, i actually like to press the keys on my keyboard to make the letters come up.
