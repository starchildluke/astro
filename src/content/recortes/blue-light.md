they tell me you're bad for me  
you'll keep me awake  
but i've never felt calmer  
in the peace that you make  

the joy of a console  
all aqua and blue  
it brings me to childhood  
right in clear view  

my dad had a rover  
in a light oxygen hue  
compact and charming  
such stillness imbued  

my bedding in navy  
is my favourite to sleep in  
cottony softness  
against my cool skin  

blue might be my second  
best colour of all  
but no light on earth  
does more to enthrall
