<Lede>I've reached another limit.</Lede> Over the last few weeks, I've felt a spike in my growing disdain for social media. This is nothing new as it's been on and off for years now. When Twitter started going downhill, I left and found a new place on Bluesky but that's gradually fallen too.

Lately, it's been the sheer volume (loudness and capacity) of people's opinions and I'm past my limit with exposure to everything everyone ever says, thinks, or does at a speed faster than I can handle. Why do I need to hear what yet another white cis male CEO has to say about anything? Why must I see everyone's dunks, blue liberal soapboxing, and attempts at humour? Why do people feel the need to post Twitter screenshots and call it [The Bad Place](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Good_Place#Premise_and_synopsis) like this is a TV show?

I've done the best I can to curate my spaces so that it's a mix of people whom I'd consider friends (even if it's one-way) and accounts that post pictures and videos that give me dopamine. I'm not interested in putting "differing opinions" into the mix because those opinions usually involve othering me or wanting me or my friends dead. I have nothing to gain from that.

I just feel like that [meme with the hand in the water seeking help](https://imgflip.com/memegenerator/168453057/High-five-drown) and another hand just gives it a high 5 and then the hand in the water just drowns. It's like if you don't join in with what everyone else is doing and saying, you just get swept away. And yes, it's silly of me to try and build connections online like that now but it used to work. I made IRL friends on Twitter that I still have. But Bluesky is just for posturing (emphasis on the 'post' part).

I'm taking a break from it all as best I can. It means any thoughts that I'd normally post I'll allow to breeze through my brain like a cloud in the sky. Thing is I've been feeling quite lonely lately so as much as I have plenty of distractions, they wear thin after a while. And when all you're doing is distracting yourself, there's a bigger issue at hand. But that's for me to figure out.

This isn't a cry for help (dunno how many times I've said that for fear of people reaching out with unhelpful advice) but more a letting it out somewhere that isn't a paper journal. If you feel the same, solidarity! And you're welcome to email with that sentiment in mind. I guess it feeds into that [weather-beaten](/posts/weather-beaten/) feeling I wrote about a few years ago.

A new platform won't fix me nor do I have the energy to start it all again. I guess if you want to reach me in a meaningful way, you'll know where to find me. And if not, take care.
