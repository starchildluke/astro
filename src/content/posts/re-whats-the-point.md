> so what's the point of all this? why post at all? why care about the open web when the hounds of capitalism bang harder on the door every day?

That's quote from [my friend Olu](https://olu.online/whats-the-point/) and as they asked for responses, here's mine.

Well, I've actually [answered this question](https://lukealexdavis.co.uk/posts/a-note-on-some-notes-on-writing/) [a few](https://lukealexdavis.co.uk/posts/blogging-as-self-expression/) [times](https://lukealexdavis.co.uk/posts/blogging-gratitude/) [before](https://lukealexdavis.co.uk/recortes/blogging-is/).

I am therefore I blog. I have so many ideas that go around in my head, some that never see the light of day—not even drafts—but a fair few make their way onto websites and that includes this one. I don't know if I have a defining universal answer as to why but I know if I feel strongly enough about something, I've got to let it out. I don't care that much if people don't read. I'm used to people ignoring me or feeling invisible. But I refuse to go out without there being a record of what I stood for and how I felt about the things I love.

One of my favourite producers has [a quote that lines the background of his Bandcamp page](https://knxwledge.bandcamp.com/) which says <q>if you don't get it off your chest, you'll never be able to breathe</q>. Pithy perhaps, but that sounds about right. Also, why the hell should I have to watch billionaires do the most heinous stuff imaginable and just come out posting absolute garbage unabashed while I feel guilty for saying I'm taking a social media break and then come back to post 2 days later? You're gonna see my post before or after you've rolled your eyes and you're gonna like it!
