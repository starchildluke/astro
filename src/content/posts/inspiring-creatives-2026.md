[In 2017, I made a list of inspiring creatives](/posts/10-inspiring-creatives-2017/) and [in 2025, I made another one](/posts/inspiring-creatives-2025/). So this year I decided to carry on the tradition.

## 1. Vincent Warmerdam

Vincent was in last year's list but he remains a big inspiration when it comes to coding and some of the deeper thoughts about how and why to build things. I often go back to his old videos and look through repos for inspo before tackling some projects.

[koaning.io](https://koaning.io/)

## 2. Joshua Briond

I don't use Instagram much these days but when I did, I lived for Joshua Briond's reels. He was on the tightrope of dense academia and sillier pop culture rhetoric to the point where I understood what he was talking about without **knowing** knowing what he was talking about. If that doesn't make any sense, don't even worry about it. Just know that he's a very thoughtful and knowledgeable person who always has great insight. I see more of his stuff on Letterboxd and I especially enjoy his reviews and how he treats the 5-star rating system.

[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/jos.hau/) | [Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/joshuabriond/) | [Taming the Revolution: White Power and the Fear of Black Freedom (book)](https://www.plutobooks.com/product/taming-the-revolution/)

## 3. Anh aka anhvn

Anh was in my 2025 list and she still inspires me through her approach to web design and beautiful sketches. I still look and get an itch to redo it all but I let the thought flow through my head like a cloud each time.

[Website](https://anhvn.com/)

## 4. Jacky Alciné

Jacky is a software engineer (whom you should hire btw!) and as I said last year, he's an important part of the space due to his thoughtfulness around people rather than just things.

[Website](https://jalcine.github.io/) | [Blacksky](https://blacksky.community/profile/jacky.blacksky.app)

## 5. jamie aka seafare

Jamie remains the GOAT. That is all.

[seafare](https://seafare.neocities.org/) | [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/gecqua.bsky.social) | [Tumblr](https://totodile.tumblr.com/)

## 6. Dom Griffin

Dom has been a friend for a while now and he has such a phenomenal way with words. He has got me into films I never would have contemplated watching and they've become some of my favourites (Nemesis, I'm looking at you). A joy to have around on the Internet and very good at what he does.

[Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/capnmagik/) | [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/thearmchairauteur/) | [Looper](https://www.looper.com/author/dominicgriffin/) | [Baltimore Beat](https://baltimorebeat.com/author/dominic-griffin/)

## 7. Ky Decker

Ky is another bright spark in the tech scene (and another person you should definitely hire, [not now but right now](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=246DWkIFib0)). I always look forward to their posts on Bluesky and on their website which is also super inspiring and awesome. Ky deserves the best and more so definitely check them out.

[Website](https://ky.fyi/) | [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/ky.fyi) | [Are.na](https://www.are.na/ky-decker/)

## 8. Taurean Bryant

I've known Taurean for a minute and he's another cool influence in tech. Super level-headed and very inquisitive, he makes you think and it's welcoming to have someone in the space who doesn't default to hype. He co-runs a design community called [3&3](https://3-3.fyi/) where people can talk about design and they host regular meetups of which I've joined a few and they've been super fun.

[Website](https://taurean.work/) | [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/taurean.bryant.land)

## 9. flashoverride

This last one might be a little strange but stay with me. I started getting into RetroAchievements around March time as a coping mechanism for various family things. One of the inspirations for that was flashoverride as he is currently the #1 player in the casual leaderboard (casual is basically a subsection where people can use cheats and save states; formerly known as softcore, in contrast to "hardcore" where cheats aren't allowed).

Anyway, he ploughs through games and openly uses cheats as he's documented on the message board on his page (seriously, it's a doozy!) What he said made me see gaming a lot differently and while I couldn't play at his rate and wouldn't want to, it allowed me to be a lot more free with how I played and I've played a ton since then and enjoyed a lot of new games I never would have experienced without his openness.

[RetroAchievements](https://retroachievements.org/user/flashoverride)
