Joining in with [James's Food Questions Challenge](https://jamesg.blog/2026/10/16/food-questions-challenge)!

### What was your favourite food growing up?

I loved the usual things: chocolate (Mars bars, [Wispa Gold](https://www.cadbury.co.uk/products/cadbury-wispa-gold-chocolate-bar-48g/), Fudge, [Milky Way](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milky_Way_(chocolate_bar)#Global_version)), pizza. Fruit wise, I loved a (red and golden delicious) and raisins (chocolate raisins were an elite combo). [Magnums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnum_(ice_cream)) were the ultimate treatment back when £1–2 for chocolate-coated ice cream on a stick was actually premium.

### What is your favourite food that is made locally? (In your region/country.)

My hometown is where [Seabrook Crisps](https://www.seabrookcrisps.com/) originated and are still made. We only used to have them at Christmas when my mum would get assorted flavours but over time they became more accessible and I'd buy them on the way home from school or my mum would get them as part of the weekly shop.

Now I buy them as a cheaper nostalgic alternative to a Walkers' multipack. Bradford represent!

### What is your favourite food generally?

I'm a pizza fiend. I judge how expensive a restaurant is by how much they charge for a margherita pizza. I'd have defied the Catholic Church and Galileo by suggesting a pizzacentric model. And don't be stingy with the cheese!

### What is one food you have had in the last year that you remember warmly?

There's a restaurant near my mum's house in the south of France where the chef makes the best steak I've ever had. I get it every time I go and he also makes something called <span lang="fr"><a href="https://cultrface.co.uk/lost-brioche-a-decadent-french-bread-based-dessert/">brioche perdu</a></span> which is like French toast and, again, pure decadence.

### What food do you really want to try?

<span lang="pt">Bacalhau</span>. I've grown up with saltfish as my mum is [Jamaican](/jardim/jamaica/) but having [visited Lisbon so many times](/jardim/lisbon/), I've still not tried <span lang="pt">bacalhau</span> so that's on the top of my list for when I go back in November.

### Do you take photos of your meals?

Yes I do. Sometimes I forget because hunger prevails (as well it should tbh) but it's nice to remember the meal afterwards.

### Savoury or sweet?

Sweet. Sometimes<sup><a href="#in-1">\*</a></sup> I put honey in my tea just to feel something.

### Spicy or no?

I prefer not to have anything spicy but a teensy bit goes a long way for me. I think too much is overrated because if all you can taste is capsaicin and flames, what's the point?

### Tea or coffee? (Both? Neither?)

Tea. I love a mocha or a ristretto but you can't beat a nice cup of tea. The best cuppas can ameliorate your soul.
