Over the last few weeks, I've been going out more. At weekends when I have my son, I've gone somewhere at least once (usually the Sunday) whether it was a nature walk, a slide park, or a random bus ride to somewhere new. It's been great and getting out of the house and away from the laptop or phone (for the most part) has helped calm my mind down.

We've also started going to a local café for a drink and some food as a change of scenery and I just got back from a solo trip an hour ago. Has it been completely offline? No; I've been on my phone but my mind has been clearer and that's a net plus for me.

And this is all because I've hated social media more and more every day. No matter how you curate your feeds, it's just wall-to-wall doom and gloom, reposts of The Worst People You've Never Heard Of Doing The Worst Things You Didn't Know Existed, shaming people into (in)action, celebrities and their lives. All my friends do it and it's tough to handle at scale because who am I to say can you stop? I think it's important to know what's going on but if that's _all_ your putting out there and into your direct orbit, how are you gonna know what Good looks like if/when we get it?

So I've gone outside and not had those apps open and glued to my eyes. When I went to the slide park, I figured he wouldn't want to just slide around on his own so I joined him and that meant putting my phone away in a locker. Best decision I ever made. The slide park was fun anyway (I kinda wanna go again next weekend even thought it cost like £50 altogether) but that disconnection was so needed.

This isn't me soapboxing about how we all need to log off and touch grass blah blah blah or pretending like I've just discovered fresh air beyond my front door but I would recommend going out there if you can and doing literally anything other than scaring yourself into a moral freeze.
