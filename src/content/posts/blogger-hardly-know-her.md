I've been in a bit of a blogging slump on my other blogs. I've just not had the motivation to write anything that wasn't personal, which is funny because that usually happens in the opposite direction. It's happened before and it'll pass but not just yet and I'm not in a rush to bring the mojo back.

I think the main blockers have been my mental health and having to deal with [this annoying move](/posts/resourceful/). For the most part it's done but I still have a few things to take care of by the end of September. Between that and work being busy and both demanding every ounce of energy I have everyday, I don't have the desire to blog about music, culture, sports, STEM, or even video games. In fact, even my non-Pokémon gaming has taken a hit. Such is modern life I guess!

If you've ever fallen into a blogging slumber, what stirred you out of it? [Email and let me know!](mailto:luke@lukealexdavis.co.uk)
